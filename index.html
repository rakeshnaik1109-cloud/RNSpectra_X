🧬 Identify — Reference Library Match
▼
Compares a processed spectrum with 202 reference spectra of 141 biomolecules from the open RamanBiolib library and ranks the closest matches. Each match shows the publication its reference spectrum comes from. A match is a suggestion to confirm, not proof of identity.
🔬 Analysis — Peak Fit · Band Ratio · PCA · Difference
▼
Fits a region as a sum of bands on a background and reports each band's centre, FWHM, height and area with standard errors — e.g. Amide I (1580–1720) or 1200–1350 cm⁻¹.
Height or area of a band — or the ratio of two bands, e.g. 1004 / 1450 — for every spectrum, with group mean ± SD and a t-test (2 groups) or ANOVA (3 or more).
Principal component analysis of the individual processed spectra. Groups (from the Group boxes) are coloured in the score plot; the loadings show which bands separate them.
Subtracts B from A point by point. Positive bands are stronger in A, negative in B; the shaded band shows the uncertainty of the difference.

🧬 Library match

▼
#SubstanceScoreReference measured bySource of reference spectrum
Click a row to overlay it. Strong / possible: correlation HQI ≥ 90 / ≥ 75; derivative HQI ≥ 55 / ≥ 30; peak match ≥ 60 / ≥ 35. A top match leading the next by ≥ 15 points is marked “clear lead”.
The reference spectra are spontaneous (non-SERS) Raman spectra of pure compounds, digitised from published figures. SERS spectra, mixtures and different excitation wavelengths change relative band intensities, so treat matches as hypotheses and confirm against a measured standard.

① Data Input
X Y pairs — optional 3rd column = Y error, 4th = X error. Choosing a data type only pre-fills the axis labels; any numeric X–Y data can be plotted and fitted.
CSV, TXT or Excel. A table with one X column and several Y columns (e.g. a spectrometer export with a wavelength column and one column per spectrum) is split into one dataset per column and plotted together. Header rows such as Int.time[ms]--> are read as file information.

▼

▼
