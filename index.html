Axis Frame, Grid & Notation
▼
Tip: Raman baselines that hover near zero can create a stray dark line at Y=0 — that line is off by default here; tick "Y=0 Line" to bring it back.
Scale Bar (Data-Accurate)
▼
The bar is drawn against the real Y axis, so its length is genuinely proportional to your data (not just a label) — useful when you'd rather show "this much = N counts" than print Y-axis numbers at all.
Inset Plot (Zoom Region)
▼
Leave Region X Min/Max blank to inset the full spectrum (rarely useful) — set a narrow window (e.g. the Amide I band) to zoom into it. Position/size are figure-fraction coordinates (0 = left/bottom edge, 1 = right/top edge).
